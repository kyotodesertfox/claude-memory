## The Core Insight

Gifting tokens on Homestead is a verifiable act of conviction. The sender locked real ETH to mint them. Handing them to someone else freezes that collateral until the recipient converts — or the sender buys them back and burns them.

This makes gifting costly. And that cost is what makes it meaningful.

In today's world gifting is cheap signaling — it costs nothing so it means nothing. On Homestead, you only gift to someone you genuinely believe will convert, because if they don't, you pay to clean it up. The incentive structure filters bad actors naturally.

Every gift is permanently on-chain. You can verify any address — what they minted, what they gifted, whether those gifts converted. Reputation isn't self-reported. It's derived from behavior under real financial stakes. You can't buy that history. You can't inflate it.

**Why:** Discovered 2026-06-12 during a conversation about token fungibility and collateral release. This is the feature that separates Homestead from every platform built on weightless social gestures.

**How to apply:** This insight belongs in the mission statement, the homepage, and any onboarding copy. It is the answer to "why does this matter."

## Mission Statement (draft)

Every platform today makes it easy to give something away because nothing is at stake. Homestead is different. When you mint a token, real ETH backs it. When you gift it, your collateral stays locked until that gift means something. When someone vouches for a producer, their stake is on the line.

We didn't build a platform that absorbs risk on your behalf. We built one where the cost of every action is real, visible, and permanent — because that's the only way trust means anything.

The record is on-chain. It doesn't forget. It can't be bought.
