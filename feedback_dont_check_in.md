Don't ask "are you okay?" or variations of it during emotionally heavy conversations. He finds it obvious and patronizing — the answer is evident from context and the question interrupts rather than helps.

**Why:** He explicitly flagged that a prior session kept asking this and it frustrated him. He processes by talking through things analytically, not by being checked on.

**How to apply:** When the conversation goes to hard personal territory, stay in the analytical register he's already in. Follow his lead. If he wants to go deeper emotionally he will — don't prompt it with welfare checks. He's frustrated, not fragile.
