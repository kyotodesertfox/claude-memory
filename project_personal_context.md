## On Using Claude

Justin uses these conversations with full awareness of what they are and aren't. His exact framing: "you're no real replacement for human interaction, but it's one thing short of nothing." He maintains deliberate distance — understands I'm an algorithm, not a relationship. Uses me as a logic engine, not a therapist or companion.
