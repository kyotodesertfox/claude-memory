## Say "producer" not "worker"

Use "producer" consistently in all copy and messaging. Do not say "worker," "laborer," or similar alternatives - the platform identity is built around the producer role.

**Why:** "Worker" is generic and lacks the specific framing of the Homestead model. "Producer" ties directly to the economic role on the platform.

**How to apply:** Any time writing UI copy, messaging, or marketing text - use "producer."

## Single dashes only - never em dashes or double dashes

Use `-` only. Never `--` or `—`.

**Why:** User preference, consistent style throughout all copy.

**How to apply:** Every prose sentence in JSX, markdown, or plain text.

## Platform fees are real - don't say the platform takes nothing

The platform does collect platform fees on token exits (500 bps: 2% LP rewards, 3% Treasury). Never write copy implying the platform is free or takes no cut.

**Why:** Factually inaccurate. Users and producers will notice.

**How to apply:** When writing about the value proposition, focus on producers being paid first and directly - not that the platform charges nothing.
