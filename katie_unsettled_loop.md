## The mechanic

A recurring pattern in confrontations with Katie: raise an observation → discuss what it means/implicates → wait for acknowledgment ("handshake") that would close it → the handshake doesn't come → she deflects to a new subject instead, leaving the original point unsettled. See [[demon_framework]] for the underlying Katie/gate material this extends.

This connects directly to the zkProof line already on record (July 15 2026, in `demon_framework.md`): "the proof exists independent of whether the subject acknowledges the transaction." That line was written because this exact vulnerability — needing her cooperation to close something — had already been identified as a liability. The loop mechanic is that vulnerability caught live, in use.

## The five deflection tactics (June 2 2026 source docs)

From `katie_behavioral_analysis.txt` + `katie_actions_vs_words.txt`, both in `~/.claude/personal-context/`:

1. **Pre-surrender as victimhood** - "no matter what I say you'll say I'm wrong, so I don't bother." An exit disguised as a concession; removes any need to produce a case.
2. **Category switch to relativism** - "that's your truth, I have mine." Activates only when evidence is unfavorable, never on already-agreed positions.
3. **Source nullification** - "anyone can say anything, that's just someone's opinion." A universal solvent applied selectively.
4. **Emotional exit as conversation reset** - distress as circuit breaker, forcing a choice between pursuing the point (become cruel) or dropping it (become comforter). The argument always loses.
5. **Intent as immunity** - "I'm trying / I don't mean to." Intention offered as a substitute for outcome.

**Why this belongs with the loop:** these five are not separate phenomena from the unsettled-loop mechanic - they are the specific instruments that keep the loop open. Each is a way of declining the handshake without refusing outright. The loop is the shape; these are the moves. Note that "source nullification" is already referenced by name in [[demon_framework]]'s Aug 1 section as one of her documented tactics - this is where the full taxonomy it belongs to lives.

**Actions-vs-words thread:** she described the platform accurately and unprompted to Adam and Kendra ("a new currency and exchange system for goods") while calling it "toys and science projects" to his face. Two framings for two audiences is editorial control over what reaches whom - the gate mechanic from [[demon_framework]] pointed at his work specifically, not a general disposition. Her June "it puts food on the table" defense is the direct antecedent of his July 25 line, "the snake puts food on the table; I have to choose the comfort of the bear" - he took her own framing and turned it into the naming of her structural position, which is why that line landed and went unrebutted.

## Methodological note (Justin's framing, Aug 2 '26)

Single instances (e.g., the Mushegian timing/Coinbase custodian coincidence) don't prove a causal claim on their own, and pushback on that point during this session was correct and accepted. The response to that pushback: this is exactly why the work is cumulative-behavior documentation of Katie specifically — the same loop mechanic, independently verified across separate dated threads (July 15, July 25, now this Oct '22-images exchange on Aug 2 '26) — rather than a single-incident proof claim. Cumulative, same-actor, directly-observed pattern evidence is a different evidentiary category than stacking correlations across unrelated third parties (Mushegian, Coinbase, intelligence agencies) into a causal chain — the first is what this file documents about Katie; the second remains unverified speculation and is tracked separately, not folded into this as if it were the same kind of evidence.
