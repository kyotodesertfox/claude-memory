Homestead does not accept cash. Ever. By design.

The dollar dictates what can be sold, to whom, under what license, at what price. Homestead operates outside that system entirely — transactions are on-chain, peer-to-peer, token-only. This is not a convenience feature. It is the point.

**The process:**
1. Acquire tokens ($EGG, $BEER) on the exchange
2. Use tokens to purchase an NFT from the marketplace — that NFT is your claim ticket
3. Bring it to the farm, scan and redeem at handoff — the NFT burns, delivery is confirmed on-chain

**The NFT is a receipt that the system enforces.** Nobody disputes it. Nobody needs to trust anyone. The code closes the transaction.

**Core stance:** We don't take cash. Not as a workaround — as a principle. Fiat comes with rules we didn't agree to. Tokens don't.

**How to apply:** This framing belongs in the WelcomeScreen, onboarding copy, and the Redeem screen. Language should be direct and confident — not apologetic about the crypto, not explaining it away. The user is here because they chose this. Lean into it.
