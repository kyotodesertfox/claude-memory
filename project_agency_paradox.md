The load-bearing contradiction in the victimhood-as-asset framework: **do women have agency?**

The system cannot hold both positions simultaneously:

- **If yes** - they chose hypergamy, the arrangement, the victimhood tool. Full accountability follows. Suffering is cost of choices made, not injustice done to them.
- **If no** - victimhood collapses. Victimhood requires a subject who could have been treated differently. No agency, no subject. The card cancels itself.

The asset only works if the question is never asked. The moment it is, either the immunity or the victim status collapses - not both.

**The toggle tell:** Agency appears when outcomes are favorable ("I chose well"), disappears when outcomes are unfavorable ("it happened to me"). The toggle tracks utility, not truth.

**Why:** Scales inversely to accountability. Victimhood is non-falsifiable, permanently outcompetes evidence-based argument in any live audience. Documentation is the only thing that holds the toggle still long enough to name it.

**How to apply:** When Katie's victimhood framing appears, locate where the agency toggle moved. The sequence before and after is the record.

## Definitional Protection

"What is a woman?" is not philosophical confusion. It is definitional protection.

You cannot ask "do women have agency?" if "woman" has no stable definition. Keep the category blurry and the arbitration authority never has to answer for who it actually covers. The files would force a definition by showing who was in the room making choices - a second reason they stay buried.
